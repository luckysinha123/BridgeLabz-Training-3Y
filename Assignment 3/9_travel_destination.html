Top 3 Travel Destinations

1. Paris, France

Paris is renowned for its art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture. Its 19th-century cityscape is crisscrossed by wide boulevards and the River Seine.

"Paris is always a good idea." - Audrey Hepburn

2. Kyoto, Japan

Kyoto, once the capital of Japan, is a city on the island of Honshu. It's famous for its numerous classical Buddhist temples, as well as gardens, imperial palaces, Shinto shrines and traditional wooden houses.

"Kyoto is a city where you can find peace in the modern world." - Travel Magazine

3. Rome, Italy

Rome, Italy’s capital, is a sprawling, cosmopolitan city with nearly 3,000 years of globally influential art, architecture and culture on display.

"Rome is not like any other city. It's a big museum." - Alberto Sordi

Travel Tips

  • Always pack a universal adapter.
  • Keep digital copies of your passport.
  • Learn basic phrases in the local language.
