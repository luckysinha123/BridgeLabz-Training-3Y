Welcome to Kyoto

Kyoto, once the capital of Japan, is a city on the island of Honshu. It's famous for its numerous classical Buddhist temples, as well as gardens, imperial palaces, Shinto shrines and traditional wooden houses.

Historical Legacy (Linear Gradient)

Founded in 794 as Heian-kyo, Kyoto served as Japan's imperial capital for over a millennium. Its centuries of historical stability allowed arts, tea ceremony traditions, and architectural marvels to flourish.

794 AD

Heian-kyo founded as the imperial capital of Japan.

1603 AD

Nijo Castle constructed during the Edo Shogunate.

1994 AD

UNESCO designates Historic Monuments of Ancient Kyoto.

Cultural Treasures (Radial Gradient)

From the serene Zen rock garden at Ryoan-ji to the golden pavilion of Kinkaku-ji, Kyoto's cultural aesthetic embodies harmony, simplicity, and natural elegance.
